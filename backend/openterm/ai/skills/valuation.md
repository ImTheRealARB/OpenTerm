# Playbook: valuation

Goal: say what a company is priced at relative to what it earns, owns and grows, and how that compares with peers and its own history.

1. `profile` for sector and industry; `quote` for price and market cap.
2. `fa` (annual, then quarterly if recent change matters): revenue, operating income, net income, free cash flow (operating cash flow minus capital expenditure), debt, cash, shares.
3. Compute from those numbers only: price to earnings, enterprise value (market cap plus debt minus cash) over operating income and over free cash flow, free cash flow margin, revenue growth over 3 years. Show the arithmetic with each input cited.
4. `research` fn `ee` for consensus estimates, `anr` for analyst ratings; `screener` with the same sector to name larger or similar peers and compare multiples that you can compute from tool data.
5. `history` over 5Y to see where the price sits against its own range.

Report: a short table-free list: what the multiples are, what they compare to, what would change the picture (growth, margins, debt). State data dates and any stale or missing tool result.

Limits: a valuation is a model, not a price target. Say which inputs are missing. This is analysis, not personalised investment advice.
