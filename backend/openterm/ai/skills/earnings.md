# Playbook: earnings

Goal: summarise what a company reported, what was expected, and what the market did.

1. `research` fn `ee` (estimates) and `evts` (events) for the next and last reporting dates and the consensus.
2. `fa` with period Q for the last four quarters: revenue, operating income, net income, margins, year over year growth. Compute growth yourself from the tool numbers and cite them.
3. `filings` with form 10-Q or 10-K (or 8-K for the release) to name the document and its date; do not quote text you did not read.
4. `news` for the last headlines about the report; `history` over 1M to see the price move around the report date.

Report: result against expectation (only if both numbers came from tools), margin trend, the price reaction with dates, and what to watch next quarter. Mark every figure with its tool call.

Limits: if consensus or the quarter is missing, say so instead of estimating. This is analysis, not personalised investment advice.
