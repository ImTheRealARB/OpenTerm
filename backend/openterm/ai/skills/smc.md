- **Long**: bullish ChoCH/BOS + bullish FVG exists

- **Stand aside**: no structural signal or conflicting directions

## Reference: break of structure

## Break of Structure (BOS) and Change of Character (ChoCH)

### BOS (Break of Structure)

A break of structure is a signal that the market trend continues:
- **Bullish BOS**: price breaks above the previous swing high, confirming that the uptrend continues.
- **Bearish BOS**: price breaks below the previous swing low, confirming that the downtrend continues.

A BOS shows that the current trend direction is still valid and that institutional money is still working in the same direction.

### ChoCH (Change of Character)

A change of character is a signal that the trend may reverse:
- **Bullish ChoCH**: in a downtrend, price breaks above the previous swing high.
- **Bearish ChoCH**: in an uptrend, price breaks below the previous swing low.

A ChoCH is a stronger signal than a BOS, because it shows a fundamental change in market structure.

### Signal weight

In this engine:
- ChoCH signals carry the highest weight (trend reversal).
- BOS signals are used for confirmation (trend continuation).
- Both are combined with the FVG filter to reduce false signals.

## Reference: order blocks and fvg

## Order Blocks and Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

### Order Blocks

An order block is an area where institutions build large positions. It usually shows up as:
- Price leaves a price area quickly.
- That area then becomes strong support or resistance.
- When price retests the order block, a reaction often follows.

#### Bullish order block
The area of the last down (bearish) candle in a downtrend, after which price rises strongly.

#### Bearish order block
The area of the last up (bullish) candle in an uptrend, after which price falls strongly.

### FVG (Fair Value Gap)

An FVG is a price gap in the middle of three candles:
- **Bullish FVG**: the low of the third candle is above the high of the first candle (a gap is left in the middle).
- **Bearish FVG**: the high of the third candle is below the low of the first candle.

An FVG shows an extreme imbalance between buying and selling pressure, and price usually comes back to "fill" the gap.

### Role in the signal

The FVG is used as a filter:
- When a bullish FVG exists, a long signal is more reliable (buyers are strong).
- When a bearish FVG exists, a short signal is more reliable (sellers are strong).
