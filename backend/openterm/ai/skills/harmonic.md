## PRZ (Potential Reversal Zone) trading

### What is the PRZ

The PRZ (Potential Reversal Zone) is a price area where several Fibonacci levels converge:
- The price area where point D completes
- The intersection of several Fibonacci projections
- A high-probability reversal area

### Trading rules

#### Entry
- Wait inside the PRZ for the price to react (a reversal candlestick pattern).
- Do not enter early, before point D is confirmed.

#### Stop loss
- Bullish pattern: place the stop below point D.
- Bearish pattern: place the stop above point D.
- Usually 1 to 1.5 times the width of the PRZ.

#### Take profit
- First target: the 0.382 retracement of the AD leg
- Second target: the 0.618 retracement of the AD leg
- Third target: point A

### Notes
- A completed harmonic pattern does not guarantee a reversal.
- Confirm with volume and candlestick patterns.
- Patterns on higher timeframes are more reliable.

## XABCD harmonic patterns

### How the patterns are recognised

A harmonic pattern is made of 5 points: X, A, B, C, D. Each leg stands in a precise Fibonacci ratio to the others.

### Gartley pattern (the "222" pattern)
- AB = 0.618 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.27-1.618 × BC
- D = 0.786 × XA (the key level)
- Bullish Gartley: X is the low and A the high; D reverses at the bottom.
- Bearish Gartley: X is the high and A the low; D reverses at the top.

### Bat pattern
- AB = 0.382-0.5 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.618-2.618 × BC
- D = 0.886 × XA (the key level)
- Point B retraces less, and point D goes deeper.

### Butterfly pattern
- AB = 0.786 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.618-2.618 × BC
- D = 1.27 × XA (beyond point X)
- The only pattern in which point D lies beyond point X.

### Crab pattern
- AB = 0.382-0.618 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 2.24-3.618 × BC
- D = 1.618 × XA (the key level, the furthest extension)
- The most extreme harmonic pattern.
