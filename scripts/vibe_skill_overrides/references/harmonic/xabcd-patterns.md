# XABCD Harmonic Patterns

## How the patterns are recognised

A harmonic pattern is made of 5 points: X → A → B → C → D, with precise Fibonacci ratios between the legs.

## Gartley pattern (the "222" pattern)
- AB = 0.618 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.27-1.618 × BC
- D = 0.786 × XA (the key ratio)
- Bullish Gartley: X low, A high, reversal up at D at the bottom
- Bearish Gartley: X high, A low, reversal down at D at the top

## Bat pattern
- AB = 0.382-0.5 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.618-2.618 × BC
- D = 0.886 × XA (the key ratio)
- Point B retraces less, point D goes deeper

## Butterfly pattern
- AB = 0.786 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.618-2.618 × BC
- D = 1.27 × XA (beyond point X)
- The only pattern whose point D goes beyond point X

## Crab pattern
- AB = 0.382-0.618 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 2.24-3.618 × BC
- D = 1.618 × XA (the key ratio, the furthest extension)
- The most extreme harmonic pattern
