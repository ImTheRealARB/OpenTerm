# XABCD harmonic patterns

## How the patterns are recognised

A harmonic pattern is made of 5 points: X, A, B, C, D. Each leg stands in a precise Fibonacci ratio to the others.

## Gartley pattern (the "222" pattern)
- AB = 0.618 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.27-1.618 × BC
- D = 0.786 × XA (the key level)
- Bullish Gartley: X is the low and A the high; D reverses at the bottom.
- Bearish Gartley: X is the high and A the low; D reverses at the top.

## Bat pattern
- AB = 0.382-0.5 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.618-2.618 × BC
- D = 0.886 × XA (the key level)
- Point B retraces less, and point D goes deeper.

## Butterfly pattern
- AB = 0.786 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 1.618-2.618 × BC
- D = 1.27 × XA (beyond point X)
- The only pattern in which point D lies beyond point X.

## Crab pattern
- AB = 0.382-0.618 × XA
- BC = 0.382-0.886 × AB
- CD = 2.24-3.618 × BC
- D = 1.618 × XA (the key level, the furthest extension)
- The most extreme harmonic pattern.
