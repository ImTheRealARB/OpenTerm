# Trading the PRZ (Potential Reversal Zone)

## What the PRZ is

The PRZ (Potential Reversal Zone) is a price area where several Fibonacci levels converge:
- The price area where point D completes
- Where several Fibonacci projections meet
- An area with a higher chance of a reversal

## Trading rules

### Entry
- Wait for the price to react inside the PRZ (a reversal candlestick pattern)
- Do not enter early, before point D is confirmed

### Stop loss
- Bullish pattern: stop below point D
- Bearish pattern: stop above point D
- Usually 1-1.5 times the width of the PRZ

### Take profit
- First target: the 0.382 retracement of the AD leg
- Second target: the 0.618 retracement of the AD leg
- Third target: point A

## Cautions
- A completed harmonic pattern does not guarantee a reversal
- Confirm with volume and candlestick patterns
- Patterns on higher timeframes are more reliable
